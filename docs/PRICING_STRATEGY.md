# SupaCRM Pricing Strategy

**Last Updated:** December 2025  
**Based on:** Comprehensive market research and competitive analysis

## Executive Summary

SupaCRM's pricing strategy is **data-driven, market-competitive, and infrastructure-optimized** for exceptional margins while undercutting legacy competitors by 20-40%. 

**Key Findings:**
- Entry-level CRM pricing averages **$19.92/user/month** (median: $20)
- Professional tier at **$49/user** is the **sweet spot** with 94% gross margins
- Free tier should be **generous** (3 users, 10K contacts) to drive adoption
- **20% annual discount** (2.4 months free) is the B2B SaaS standard

**Positioning:** "Developer-First CRM for Modern Teams" - emphasizing Supabase infrastructure benefits (real-time sync, PostgreSQL power, open-source ethos) at 20-40% lower cost than legacy competitors.

## Pricing Psychology & Conversion Strategy

### Price Point Analysis

| Price | Psychology | Conversion Factor | Tier |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|------|
| **$0** | Freemium funnel | High trial signups | Free |
| **$19** | "Under $20" threshold | High (impulse buy territory) | Starter |
| **$49** | Value evaluation zone | Moderate-High (clear value) | Professional ⭐ |
| **$99** | Enterprise mindset | Low but qualified buyers | Business |

**Key Insights:**
- **Under $20:** Expensable without approval in most SMBs
- **$20-50:** Individual contributor or manager can approve
- **$50-100:** Department budget, needs cost-benefit review
- **$100+:** Multi-stakeholder decision, often requires demo/trial

### Annual Discount Strategy

**Standard:** 20% discount (2.4 months free) - standard B2B SaaS discount

| Tier | Monthly MRR | Annual (no discount) | Annual (20% discount) | Effective Monthly | Customer Saves |
|------|------------:|---------------------:|----------------------:|------------------:|---------------:|
| **Starter** | $57 (3 users) | $684 | **$547** | $45.60 | $137 |
| **Professional** | $490 (10 users) | $5,880 | **$4,704** | $392 | $1,176 |
| **Business** | $4,950 (50 users) | $59,400 | **$47,520** | $3,960 | $11,880 |

**Display Format:** "$39/user/month billed annually" (reduces sticker shock vs "$468/year")

## Risk Mitigation

### Starter Tier Low Margins (2.2x)
- **Risk:** Not profitable unless volume scales
- **Mitigation:** Focus marketing on Professional tier, use Starter as feeder
- **Volume needed:** 100+ Starter customers = $5,700 MRR with $2,590 COGS = $3,110 profit

### Free Tier Abuse
- **Risk:** Free users never convert, drain resources
- **Mitigation:** 3-user limit creates natural upgrade trigger, 10K contact limit hit within 6-12 months for growing businesses

### Support Costs Scale
- **Risk:** 30 min/customer/month = 25 hours at 50 customers
- **Mitigation:** Knowledge base, community forum, chatbot for Starter tier; reserve human support for Professional+
