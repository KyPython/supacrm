## ✅ Phase 6: Validation

### Test Your Pricing:

- [ ] **Price Anchoring Test:**
  - Does Enterprise tier make Business look reasonable?
  - Does Business tier make Professional look affordable?

- [ ] **Value Proposition:**
  - Is pricing 5-10x your costs? (healthy margin)
  - Is pricing competitive vs alternatives?
  - Is pricing accessible to target market?

- [ ] **Conversion Test:**
  - Will free tier users upgrade?
  - Is upgrade path clear?
  - Are price jumps reasonable? (not too big/small)

## Quick Research Command

Run this in Perplexity:

```
Research CRM pricing for 2024: Compare HubSpot, Pipedrive, Monday.com, Airtable, Salesforce Essentials. Include: free tier limits, paid tier pricing, per-user vs flat-rate models, typical storage limits, and average entry-level pricing. Format as comparison tables.
```

Then follow up with:

```
For a Supabase-powered CRM targeting small businesses (1-50 employees), what pricing strategy would be competitive? Consider: infrastructure costs (Supabase Pro $25/mo, Vercel Pro $20/mo), target market budget, and competitive positioning. Recommend specific price points for Free, Starter, Professional, and Business tiers.
```
